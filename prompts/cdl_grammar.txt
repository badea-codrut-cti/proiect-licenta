## Shape & Entity Declarations

2D Shapes:
Point(A): Declares a point A.
Point(A, x, y): Declares a point A at coordinates (x, y) in 2D.
Point(A, x, y, z): Declares a point A at coordinates (x, y, z) in 3D.
Line(A, B): Declares a line segment between A and B.
Angle(A, B, C): Declares an angle with vertex B.
Triangle(A, B, C): Declares a triangle formed by A, B, and C.
Polygon(A, B, C, ...): N sided polygon.
Convex(Polygon(A, B, C, D)): Polygon ABCD is convex.
Concave(Polygon(A, B, C, D)): Polygon ABCD is concave.
ObtuseTriangle(A, B, C): Declares an obtuse-angled triangle (one angle > 90deg).
AcuteTriangle(A, B, C): Declares an acute-angled triangle (all angles < 90deg).
RightTriangle(A, B, C): Declares a right-angled triangle (one angle = 90deg).
Parallelogram(J, K, L, M): Declares a parallelogram.
Square(A, B, C, D): Declares a square.
Rhombus(A, B, C, D): Declares a rhombus.
Rectangle(A, B, C, D): Declares a rectangle.
Trapezoid(Line(A, B), Line(C, D)): Trapezoid with basis AB and CD, AB and CD are parallel.
Circle(K): Declares a circle with center K.
Arc(M, N): Declares an arc between points M and N.
Centroid(Triangle(A, B, C), G): G is the centroid of triangle ABC (intersection of medians).
Circumcenter(Triangle(A, B, C), O): O is the circumcenter of triangle ABC (intersection of perpendicular bisectors, center of circumcircle).
Orthocenter(Triangle(A, B, C), H): H is the orthocenter of triangle ABC (intersection of altitudes).
Incenter(Triangle(A, B, C), I): I is the incenter of triangle ABC (intersection of angle bisectors, center of incircle).

3D Shapes:
Pyramid(A, B, C, D): Declares a triangular pyramid (tetrahedron) with base triangle ABC and apex D.
RectangularPyramid(A, B, C, D, E): Declares a rectangular pyramid with base ABCD and apex E.
Cube(A, B, C, D, E, F, G, H): Declares a cube with vertices A-H.
Parallelepiped(A, B, C, D, E, F, G, H): Declares a parallelepiped (6-faced polyhedron with parallel opposite faces). A-D form one face, E-H the opposite face, with AE, BF, CG, DH as edges.
Sphere(K): Declares a sphere with center K.
Cylinder(K): Declares a cylinder (K is the center of one base).
Cone(K): Declares a cone (K is the center of the base).
Prism(Polygon(A, B, C, D, E), Polygon(A', B', C', D' E')).

## Structural Relations

InsideOf(X, Shape): X is inside the interior of the given shape (point inside triangle, triangle inside circle, etc.).
InsideOf(Triangle(A, B, C), Circle(K)): Triangle ABC is entirely inside circle K.
InsideOf(Point(M), Triangle(A, B, C)): Point M is inside triangle ABC.
InsideOf(Point(M), Parallelogram(A, B, C, D)): Point M is inside the parallelogram ABCD.
InsideOf(Point(M), Circle(K)): Point M is inside circle K (not on the circumference).
InsideOf(Point(M), Square(A, B, C, D)): Point M is inside square ABCD.
InsideOf(Figure1, Figure2): Figure1 is entirely inside Figure2. Use when a smaller figure (e.g., triangle, square, circle) is drawn inside a larger one.
InsideOf(Point(M), Angle(A, O, B)): Line OM is between OA and OB.

PointOnLine(D, Line(A, C)): Point D is on the line segment AC.
PointOnCircle(M, Circle(K)): Point M is on the circumference of circle K.
PointOnSphere(M, Sphere(K)): Point M is on the surface of sphere K.
Parallel(X, Y): X is parallel to Y. Can be any pair of: line, plane, face, surface.
Perpendicular(X, Y): X is perpendicular to Y. Can be any pair of: line, plane, face, surface.
Congruent(Triangle(A, B, C), Triangle(D, E, F)): The two triangles are congruent.
Congruent(Angle(A, B, C), Angle(D, E, F)): The two angles are congruent (equal measure).
Similar(Triangle(A, B, C), Triangle(D, E, F)): The triangles share the same angles.
Bisector(Line(B, M), Angle(A, B, C)): Line BM bisects angle ABC.
Midpoint(M, Line(A, B)): M is the midpoint of line AB.
Circumcircle(Circle(K), Triangle(A, B, C)): Circle K passes through triangle ABC vertices.
Altitude(Line(A, D), Triangle(A, B, C)): Line AD is an altitude of triangle ABC (dropped from vertex A perpendicular to side BC).
TangentTo(Line(M, N), Circle(K)): Line MN is tangent to circle K.
Coplanar(Point(A), Point(B), Point(C), Point(D), ...): N objects are on the same plane (lines, points, etc).

## Semantic Measurements

Equals(LengthOf(Line(M, N)), 16): The length of line MN is 16.
Equals(LengthOf(Line(C, D)), x): The length of line CD is the variable x.
Equals(Radius(Circle(K)), 4): Circle K has a radius of 4.
Equals(MeasureOf(Angle(C, A, B)), 54): Angle CAB is 54 degrees.
Equals(MeasureOf(Angle(Line(O, M), Line(A, C'))), 60): The angle between the line parallel with AC' that intersects with OM is 60 degrees.
Equals(MeasureOf(Arc(M, N)), 98): The arc MN is 98 degrees.
Equals(PerimeterOf(Triangle(A, B, C)), 40): The perimeter is 40.
Equals(AreaOf(Square(A, B, C, D)), 100): The area is 100.
Equals(VolumeOf(Cube(A, B, C, D, E, F, G, H)), 27): The volume is 27.
Equals(VolumeOf(Sphere(K)), 100): The volume is 100.
Equals(AreaOf(Triangle(A, B, C)), 20): The area of the triangle is 20.
Equals(SurfaceAreaOf(Pyramid(A, B, C, D)), 50): The surface area is 50.
EqualsRatio(LengthOf(Line(A, B)), LengthOf(Line(C, D)), 2): AB / CD = 2 (AB is twice CD).
EqualsRatio(LengthOf(Line(A, D)), LengthOf(Line(A, B)), 3): AD / AB = 3.
EqualsRatio(MeasureOf(Angle(A, B, C)), MeasureOf(Angle(D, E, F)), 2): Angle ABC is twice angle DEF.
Equals(X, Sum(Y, Z)): X equals Y + Z. Example: Equals(LengthOf(Line(A, B)), Sum(LengthOf(Line(A, D)), LengthOf(Line(D, C)))) means AB = AD + DC.
Equals(Prod(LengthOf(Line(A, B)), LengthOf(Line(C, D))), Prod(LengthOf(Line(M, N)), LengthOf(Line(E, F)))): AB * CD = MN * EF.
Equals(X, Diff(Y, Z)): X equals Y - Z.
NotEquals(X, Y): X is not equal to Y.
Not(K): The statement K is not true.
Not(InsideOf(Point(M), Angle(A, O, B))): Point M is not inside angle AOB.
GreaterThan(X, Y): X > Y.
LowerThan(X, Y): X < Y.
GreaterThanEquals(X, Y): X >= Y.
LowerThanEquals(X, Y): X <= Y.
Constant(a)
IsPrime(Constant(a), true)
Equals(Constant(a), Sum(LengthOf(Line(A, B)), LengthOf(Line(C, D)))): a equals the sum of lengths AB and CD.
Equals(Prod(Constant(a), MeasureOf(Angle(A, B, C))), Sum(Prod(Constant(b), MeasureOf(Angle(D, E, F))), Prod(Constant(c), MeasureOf(Angle(G, H, I))))): a*∠ABC = b*∠DEF + c*∠GHI.
Latex(formula): Represents an arbitrary LaTeX mathematical expression. Can be used in Equals predicates:
- Equals(LengthOf(Line(A, B)), Latex(\sqrt[3]{37})): AB equals the cube root of 37.
- Equals(MeasureOf(Angle(A, B, C)), Latex(\frac{\pi}{4})): Angle ABC equals pi/4 radians.
- Equals(LengthOf(Line(A, B)), Latex(x^2 + 2x + 1))
- Equals(Constant(a), Latex(\sqrt[3]{62}))


## Handling Unknown/Unrepresentable Content

If part of the diagram or problem cannot be represented using the predicates above, use the `Unknown` marker.

Unknown(reason): Indicates something that cannot be represented.

For 3D shapes not explicitly listed above (e.g., prism with non-rectangular base, truncated shapes, composite solids), use `Unknown` with a description of the shape if it cannot be decomposed into the supported 3D primitives.
